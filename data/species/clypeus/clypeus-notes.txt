Grows on Rocky and High Metal Content bodies with a thin carbon dioxide or water atmosphere, 190 K or warmer, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Clypeus lacrimam: Rocky.
Clypeus margaritus: High Metal Content.
Clypeus speculumi: Rocky, at least 2,500 Ls from the star (rarely down to about 2,000 Ls).
Colony range: 150 m between samples.
Colour follows the parent star: A Orange, F Mauve, G Amethyst, K Grey, M Turquoise, L Teal, neutron star Yellow.
Tip: on a Rocky body check the distance from the star; beyond 2,500 Ls both lacrimam and speculumi are possible.
