Grows on Rocky and High Metal Content bodies with a thin atmosphere, gravity up to 0.275 g (Capillum also on Rocky Ice).
Carbon dioxide, by temperature: Tussock pennata 145-155 K, ventusa 155-160 K, ignis 160-170 K, serrati 170-175 K, albata 175-180 K, caputus 180-190 K, triticum 190-195 K.
Carbon dioxide, any temperature up to 195 K: Tussock pennatis and Tussock propagito.
Argon or methane: Tussock capillum (mostly on Rocky Ice).
Ammonia: Tussock catena, cultro and divisa.
Sulphur dioxide: Tussock stigmasis.
Water: Tussock virgam.
Colony range: 200 m between samples.
Colour follows the parent star: F Yellow, G Lime, K Green, M Emerald, L Sage, T Teal, Y Lime, Wolf-Rayet Orange, white dwarf Maroon, black hole Red.
Tip: on carbon dioxide worlds the surface temperature narrows it to a few species; look on plains and low slopes.
