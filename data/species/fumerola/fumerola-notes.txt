Grows at fumaroles: bodies with active volcanism and geological signals, Icy, Rocky Ice, Rocky or High Metal Content, any thin atmosphere, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Water volcanism: Fumerola aquatis (Icy, Rocky Ice, Rocky).
Carbon or methane volcanism: Fumerola carbosis (Icy, Rocky Ice).
Silicate, iron or rocky volcanism: Fumerola extremus (Rocky, High Metal Content, Rocky Ice).
Nitrogen or ammonia volcanism: Fumerola nitris (Icy, Rocky Ice).
Colony range: 100 m between samples.
Colour follows a material on the body (cadmium, mercury, molybdenum, niobium, tungsten, tin and others), not the star.
Tip: land near the geological sites; the plants grow on the vents, often in clusters on flat volcanic ground.
