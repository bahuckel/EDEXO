Grows on Rocky bodies (Tectonicas on High Metal Content) with a thin atmosphere at 165 K or warmer; no gravity rule except for Tectonicas.
Sulphur dioxide, 165 K and up: Stratum araneamus.
Sulphur dioxide or carbon dioxide, 165-190 K: Stratum excutitus and Stratum limaxus (excutitus also on oxygen).
Sulphur dioxide, carbon dioxide or oxygen, 190 K and up: Stratum cucumisis and Stratum frigus.
Ammonia, 165 K and up: Stratum laminamus.
Ammonia, water, carbon dioxide or oxygen, 165 K and up: Stratum paleas.
High Metal Content, under oxygen, ammonia, water, carbon dioxide, sulphur dioxide or argon-rich, 165 K and up, gravity up to 0.61 g: Stratum tectonicas.
Colony range: 500 m between samples.
Colour follows the parent star: F Emerald, K Lime, M Green, L Turquoise, T Grey, T Tauri Amethyst, Y Indigo, Wolf-Rayet Red, white dwarf Mauve.
Tip: on a Rocky sulphur dioxide or carbon dioxide world, 190 K splits excutitus and limaxus (below) from cucumisis and frigus (above).
