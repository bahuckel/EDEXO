Grows on Rocky and High Metal Content bodies with a thin atmosphere, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Carbon dioxide, 180-195 K: Cactoida cortexum and Cactoida pullulanta.
Ammonia, no temperature rule: Cactoida lapis and Cactoida peperatis.
Water, or rarely sulphur dioxide: Cactoida vermis.
Species on the same line share the same conditions, so the body cannot tell them apart.
Colony range: 300 m between samples.
Colour follows the parent star: A Green, F Yellow, G Teal, M Amethyst, L Mauve, T Orange, T Tauri Red, neutron star Sage.
