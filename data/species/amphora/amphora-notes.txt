Grows only on airless Metal Rich bodies at 950 K or hotter with major volcanism (metallic magma, silicate vapour geysers or rocky magma).
The system needs an A or B star; any star in the system counts, and the main star is usually A.
The system also needs at least one of: Earth-like world, Ammonia world, gas giant with water-based life, gas giant with ammonia-based life, Water giant.
One species: Amphora Plant.
Colony range: 100 m between samples.
Tip: the companion body can be anywhere in the system; a gas giant with water-based life is the usual one.
