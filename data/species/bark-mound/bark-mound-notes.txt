Grows on bodies with active volcanism: Rocky, High Metal Content, Metal Rich, Icy or Rocky Ice, 89-440 K; no gravity rule.
Most are airless; a few have a thin carbon dioxide, sulphur dioxide, carbon dioxide-rich or argon-rich atmosphere.
Grows near nebulae: 93 % of known Bark Mound systems are within 150 ly of a nebula, and all within 300 ly.
One species: Bark Mounds.
Colony range: 100 m between samples.
Tip: far from any nebula Bark Mounds are unlikely, even when the body fits.
