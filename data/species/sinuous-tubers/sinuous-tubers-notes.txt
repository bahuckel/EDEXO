Grows on Rocky and High Metal Content bodies with volcanism, 200-500 K, airless or with a thin carbon dioxide or sulphur dioxide atmosphere; no gravity rule.
Found towards the galactic core, within about 21,000 ly of Sagittarius A*.
Albidum, Caeruleum, Prasinum and Roseum: mostly within 10,000 ly of Sagittarius A*.
Blatteum, Lindigoticum, Violaceum and Viride: a ring around the core, mostly 12,000-15,000 ly out (6,000-21,000 ly).
Prasinum and Roseum also grow under thin argon-rich or oxygen (Prasinum also argon); Roseum also on Rocky Ice.
Each species is one colour; which one grows depends on planet type, volcanism, temperature, atmosphere and region.
Colony range: 100 m between samples.
Tip: beyond about 21,000 ly from the core Sinuous Tubers are unlikely.
