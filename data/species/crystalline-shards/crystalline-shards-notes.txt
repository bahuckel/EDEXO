Grows on cold Icy, Rocky, High Metal Content and Rocky Ice bodies, 20-273 K, with some volcanism (usually minor); never Metal Rich.
Most bodies are airless; thin neon, neon-rich, helium, methane, argon, argon-rich or carbon dioxide atmospheres are possible (carbon dioxide only up to 0.01 atm).
The body must be at least 12,000 Ls from the system's arrival (main) star.
The system needs a companion: Earth-like world, Ammonia world, gas giant with water- or ammonia-based life, Water giant or Water world.
Host star A, F, G, K, M, S or a brown dwarf; never O, B, white dwarf, neutron star or black hole.
One species: Crystalline Shards.
Colony range: 100 m between samples.
Tip: measure the 12,000 Ls from the arrival star, not from the star the body orbits.
