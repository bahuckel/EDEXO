Grows on Rocky, Icy, Rocky Ice and High Metal Content bodies with a thin atmosphere, gravity up to 0.28 g.
Argon, argon-rich or nitrogen: Fungoida bullarum.
Ammonia or methane: Fungoida setisis.
Carbon dioxide at 180-195 K, or water: Fungoida gelata and Fungoida stabitis (on methane or ammonia only with volcanism).
Colony range: 300 m between samples.
Colour follows a material on the body: cadmium, mercury, molybdenum, niobium, tungsten, tin, antimony, polonium, ruthenium, technetium, tellurium or yttrium.
Tip: look on high ground: mountains, cliffs, canyon walls and plateau edges.
