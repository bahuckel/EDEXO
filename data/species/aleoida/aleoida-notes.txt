Grows on Rocky and High Metal Content bodies with a thin carbon dioxide or ammonia atmosphere, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Carbon dioxide, 175-180 K: Aleoida arcus.
Carbon dioxide, 180-190 K: Aleoida coronamus.
Carbon dioxide, 190-195 K: Aleoida gravis.
Ammonia, no temperature rule: Aleoida laminiae and Aleoida spica (same conditions, the body cannot tell them apart).
Colony range: 150 m between samples.
Colour follows the parent star: B Yellow, A Green, F Teal, K Turquoise, M Emerald, L Lime, T Sage, T Tauri Mauve, Y Teal, neutron star Ocher.
Tip: on a carbon dioxide world the surface temperature alone tells you which Aleoida to expect.
