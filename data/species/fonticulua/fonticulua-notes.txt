Grows on Icy and Rocky Ice bodies with a thin atmosphere, gravity up to 0.28 g; no temperature rule.
Each species has its own atmosphere:
Campestris: argon (50 % argon or more).
Upupam: argon-rich (under 50 % argon, the rest usually nitrogen).
Segmentatus: neon-rich (a trace of neon in nitrogen air).
Lapida: nitrogen. Digitos: methane or methane-rich. Fluctus: oxygen.
Colony range: 500 m between samples.
Colour follows the parent star: O Grey, B Lime, A Green, F Yellow, G Teal, K Emerald, M Amethyst, L Mauve, T Orange, T Tauri Red, Y Ocher, Wolf-Rayet Indigo, white dwarf Turquoise, neutron star Sage, Herbig Ae/Be Maroon.
Tip: check the argon share in the atmosphere composition; above 50 % means campestris, below means upupam.
