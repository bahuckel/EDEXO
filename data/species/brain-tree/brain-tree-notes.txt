Grows on bodies with volcanism, airless or with a thin sulphur dioxide, carbon dioxide or helium atmosphere.
Most species need a companion in the system: Earth-like world, Water world, Ammonia world, Water giant, or gas giant with water- or ammonia-based life. Lividum and Roseum do not.
Aureum: Metal Rich or High Metal Content, 300-500 K.
Ostrinum and Puniceum: Metal Rich or High Metal Content, no temperature rule.
Lindigoticum: High Metal Content or Rocky, 300-500 K.
Gypseeum: Rocky, 200-330 K.
Lividum: Rocky, 225-500 K.
Viride: Icy or Rocky Ice, 100-220 K.
Roseum: any of those planet types, 200-500 K, and more atmospheres (also argon, oxygen and water).
Colony range: 100 m between samples.
Tip: often found near Guardian ruins, in clustered fields inside craters.
