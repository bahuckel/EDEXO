Grows where the thin atmosphere contains sulphur dioxide (sulphur dioxide, carbon dioxide or oxygen atmospheres), on Icy, Rocky Ice, Rocky and High Metal Content bodies, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Recepta conditivus: mostly on Icy bodies.
Recepta deltahedronix: mostly on Rocky bodies.
Recepta umbrux: on Icy and Rocky bodies alike.
Nothing on the body tells the three apart; the lines above are only where each is found most.
Colony range: 150 m between samples.
Colour follows a material on the body: antimony, polonium, ruthenium, technetium, tellurium, yttrium, cadmium, mercury, molybdenum, niobium, tungsten or tin.
Tip: check the atmosphere composition; no sulphur dioxide, no Recepta.
