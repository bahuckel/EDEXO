Grows on Rocky, High Metal Content and Rocky Ice bodies with a thin atmosphere, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Carbon dioxide or carbon dioxide-rich, by temperature: Osseus fractus 180-190 K, Osseus pellebantus 190-195 K, Osseus cornibus 180-195 K.
Water, water-rich or argon: Osseus discus.
Methane, argon or nitrogen: Osseus pumice.
Ammonia: Osseus spiralis.
Colony range: 800 m between samples: fly, don't walk.
Colour follows the parent star: O Grey, B Lime, A Green, F Yellow, G Teal, K Emerald, M Amethyst, L Orange, T Red, T Tauri Blue, Y Brown, Wolf-Rayet Indigo, white dwarf White.
Tip: look on rocky outcrops and ridges; they look like stone until you are close.
