Grows on Rocky and High Metal Content bodies with a thin atmosphere, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Ammonia, Rocky: Frutexa flabellum and Frutexa flammasis.
Carbon dioxide, Rocky, below 195 K: Frutexa acus and Frutexa fera.
Sulphur dioxide, Rocky or High Metal Content: Frutexa collum.
Water, Rocky: Frutexa sponsae.
High Metal Content with ammonia, carbon dioxide (below 195 K) or water, up to 400 K: Frutexa metallicum.
Colony range: 150 m between samples.
Colour follows the parent star: B Lime, F Green, G Emerald, M Grey, L Teal, T Tauri Mauve, white dwarf Indigo, neutron star Red.
Tip: on a High Metal Content body only metallicum and collum are possible.
