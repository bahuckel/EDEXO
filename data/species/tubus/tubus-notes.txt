Grows on Rocky and High Metal Content bodies with a thin atmosphere, very low gravity (up to 0.15 g) and 160-195 K.
Carbon dioxide, Rocky, 160-195 K: Tubus cavas, Tubus compagibus and Tubus conifer.
Ammonia, Rocky, 160-190 K: Tubus rosarium.
High Metal Content, carbon dioxide or ammonia, 160-190 K: Tubus sororibus.
Colony range: 800 m between samples: fly, don't walk.
Colour follows the parent star: B Emerald, A Indigo, F Grey, G Red, K Maroon, M Teal, L Turquoise, T Mauve, T Tauri Ocher, neutron star Amethyst.
Tip: gravity above 0.15 g rules Tubus out; on a fitting body look on flat plains and low-relief ground.
