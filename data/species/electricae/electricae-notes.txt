Grows on Icy bodies with a thin helium, neon or argon atmosphere, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Both species share those body conditions; the star or a nebula decides which one grows.
Electricae pluma: depends on the parent star; A stars (luminosity V or higher) and neutron stars. O, B, white dwarf and black hole hosts are listed but unconfirmed.
Electricae radialem: near nebulae, including small planetary nebulae, not only the big named ones.
Colony range: 1,000 m between samples: fly, don't walk.
Colour follows a material on the body: antimony, polonium, ruthenium, technetium, tellurium or yttrium.
Tip: often near frozen lakes or ice fissures, where the ice is thin or fractured.
