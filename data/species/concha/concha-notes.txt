Grows on Rocky and High Metal Content bodies with a thin atmosphere, gravity up to 0.275 g.
Ammonia: Concha aureolas.
Nitrogen: Concha biconcavis.
Carbon dioxide or carbon dioxide-rich, up to 195 K: Concha labiata. When it grows on a moon, the moon nearly always orbits within 28 Ls of its planet.
Concha renibus: carbon dioxide at 180-195 K, or water, water-rich or (rarely) ammonia; it needs volcanism or a temperature of 180 K or more.
Colony range: 150 m between samples.
Colour of aureolas and labiata follows the parent star: A Teal, F Grey, G Turquoise, K Red, L Orange, neutron star Emerald.
Colour of biconcavis and renibus follows a material on the body, not the star.
